The announcement today at the Carbon Markets Africa Summit in Kigali marks a key milestone in the development of the platform which is seeking to reach first close in 2027 at USD 20 million with a cornerstone commitment from FSDAi of GBP 5 million (USD 6.6 million).

Sineng Electric Supports Zambia’s 100 MW Maamba Solar PV Project, Advancing Solar Energy in Africa

Sineng Electric is supporting Zambia’s renewable energy development with its high-efficiency central inverters deployed at the 100 MW Maamba PV Power Station in Southern Province, which was commissioned on September 24, 2026. The project will add utility-scale solar generation capacity to the national grid, supporting the diversification of Zambia’s energy mix and enhancing its long-term energy security. Located in Maamba, Sinazongwe District, the project is being constructed by Sinoma Energy Conservation Ltd., which serves as the engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) contractor.
